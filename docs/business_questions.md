# Business Questions Registry

## Overview
This document registers the 20 strategic business questions designed for NovaPharm management decision-making, mapped to their technical SQL solutions and visual analytics components.

---

### Category 1: Market Share & Dominance
1. **Overall Portfolio Share:** What is NovaPharm's overall revenue market share for 2024 and 2025 across global markets?
2. **Therapeutic Share:** What is our market share across each Therapeutic Area, and which TA drives the highest volume?
3. **Product Ranking:** How do individual products rank in revenue and market share performance?
4. **Competitor Trajectory:** Which competitors are gaining market share at NovaPharm's expense?

---

### Category 2: Evolution & Growth Dynamics
5. **YoY Growth:** Which products achieved the highest Year-over-Year revenue growth from 2024 to 2025?
6. **Evolution Index:** How has each product evolved compared to the base period (Jan 2024 = 100)?
7. **MoM Trajectory:** What is the month-over-month growth trajectory of key oncology products (OncoShield 100mg)?
8. **Seasonality Impact:** How does respiratory product demand fluctuate between winter peak and summer off-peak?

---

### Category 3: Regional & Patient Penetration
9. **Regional Penetration:** Which global regions exhibit the highest patient penetration rate?
10. **Expansion Targets:** Which markets represent high addressable patient populations but under-indexed penetration (Priority Expansion)?
11. **Regional Ranking:** What is NovaPharm's top-performing product in each region based on prescription volume?

---

### Category 4: Pricing & Competitive Intelligence
12. **Price Index Benchmark:** How does NovaPharm's product pricing compare against competitor market averages?
13. **Price Penalty Risk:** Which products are priced > 5% above competitors and losing market share?
14. **Branded vs Generic Mix:** How does Branded product margin/revenue contribution compare against Generic lines?

---

### Category 5: Strategic Portfolio Allocation
15. **Pareto 80/20 Analysis:** Which core products generate 80% of total company revenue?
16. **BCG Quadrant Matrix:** How are products categorized across Market Share and YoY Growth (Stars, Cash Cows, Question Marks, Dogs)?
17. **Rolling 3-Month Trend:** What is the 3-month rolling revenue average trend by therapeutic area?
18. **Prescription Efficiency:** What is the average revenue generated per prescription across market tiers?
19. **Market Share Gainers vs Losers:** Which products gained or lost net market share percentage points from 2024 to 2025?
20. **Investment Allocation Matrix:** What specific strategic recommendation (Invest, Defend, Optimize Pricing, Expand Regionally, Monitor) should be assigned to each product?
