# Pharmaceutical Market & Commercial Analytics Domain Primer

## 1. Structure of Pharmaceutical Markets
The pharmaceutical industry operates differently from traditional fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) or consumer tech industries. Demand is driven by **three distinct stakeholders**:
1. **Prescribers (Physicians/Doctors):** Decide which product is selected.
2. **Patients (End Users):** Consume the therapy.
3. **Payers (Insurance / Health Authorities):** Fund or reimburse the treatment cost.

---

## 2. Product Lifecycles & Patent Status
* **Innovator / Branded Drugs:** Patent-protected therapies with exclusive marketing rights (typically 10–15 years post-approval). Characterized by high margins, premium pricing, and heavy clinical sales promotion.
* **Loss of Exclusivity (LOE) / Patent Expiry:** When patent protection expires, generic manufacturers enter the market.
* **Generic Drugs:** Chemically identical bioequivalent products sold at significant discounts (40%–80% lower price). Causes **Generic Erosion**, where innovator market share drops rapidly unless protected by line extensions (e.g., once-daily formulation, combination therapies).

---

## 3. Key Pharmaceutical Metrics Explained
* **Prescriptions Filled (Rx):** The total number of doctor prescriptions dispensed by retail or hospital pharmacies. Serves as the primary indicator of physician adoption and brand preference.
* **Addressable Patient Population (Epidemiology):** The total number of diagnosed patients within a specific therapeutic area eligible for drug treatment.
* **Patient Penetration Rate:** The percentage of addressable patients actively receiving a specific drug. High addressable population with low penetration represents an untapped market expansion opportunity.
* **Evolution Index (EI):** Standard commercial index used by pharma data providers (such as IQVIA / Ex-IMS Health) to track monthly performance vs a baseline period (Base Month = 100).
* **Price Index (PI):** Measures brand price positioning relative to market average or leading generic competitors.

---

## 4. Therapeutic Area Landscape
* **Oncology:** Cancer treatments. Characterized by high unit price, rapid innovation, strict regulatory pathways, and strong revenue growth.
* **Cardiovascular:** Heart & blood pressure medications. High volume, mature market, high generic penetration, lower margins per unit.
* **Diabetes / Endocrinology:** Metabolic health therapies (GLP-1s, SGLT2 inhibitors, Insulins). High prevalence, rapid growth in novel drug classes.
* **Respiratory:** Asthma & COPD treatments. Highly seasonal (winter surges), combination inhalers, specialized devices.
* **Central Nervous System (CNS):** Mental health, neurology, anti-depressants. Chronic therapies with high patient compliance requirements.
